Landmarks

Famous buildings are real 3D models turned into blocks, one block per metre, so a tower is a tower and not a box. Search by name, open one in your browser, or copy the command to go there in the game.

Models: Open Landmarks (CC BY 4.0) and Landmarks by Alex Wohlbruck (CC0). Where a landmark is in both, the Open Landmarks one is used. Places come from OpenStreetMap data (ODbL). In the game, /tpll takes you to the exact spot.
