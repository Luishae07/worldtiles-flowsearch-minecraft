What you can do

It's a world first, and a game on top of it.

Go anywhere

Type a place name and you're there: the Eiffel Tower, the Burj Khalifa, a street in your own town. Real terrain, real buildings, real trees.

Real buildings

Microsoft's building footprints worldwide, plus Berlin's and the Netherlands' official 3D city models. Roof colours come from satellite pictures.

Fly and ride

Board a real flight from a real airport and walk around in the cabin. Ride the trains on the real Berlin timetable.

Earn and build

Drive a taxi, work a shift, buy a house, run a shop or a hotel, fill a datacenter with racks.

Football always on

A match is always being played in the stadium. Go and watch.

Real weather

The sky, rain and snow follow the real weather, so it's snowing where it really snows.

A few commands

Type these in chat.

/go Matterhorn go to any place by name
/tpll 52.52 13.40 go to coordinates
/where where you are on Earth
/fly 5 fly at speed 1 to 10
/ride take a flight
/work start a job
/buy buy what you're looking at
/money your coins
/datacenter go to a datacenter
/credits where the data comes from
